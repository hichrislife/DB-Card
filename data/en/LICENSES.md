# Sources and licences (en)

| Source | Legal basis | Allowed licence tag | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| US federal government exam material | 17 U.S.C. § 105: works of the US federal government are public domain | `public-domain` | State-level material is NOT covered; check each state |
| UK exam boards (AQA, OCR, Edexcel, WJEC) | Copyright held by the board | **permission required** | Most boards allow classroom use only |
| Open question banks (OpenStax, CC-licensed MOOCs) | Explicit CC licence | `CC-BY-4.0`, `CC-BY-SA-4.0` | Keep the attribution string in `source` |
| MMLU / MMLU-Pro items | MIT licence | do not copy | Reference them, do not duplicate them here |
